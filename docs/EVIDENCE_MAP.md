# Evidence map

What this project shows for a junior data scientist role, and where to check it.
Each row links to code, data or results that a reviewer can open or rerun. The
last column says what is not done yet.

| Requirement | Evidence | Not done yet |
|---|---|---|
| **Python for data work** | The `reviewnlp` package: [data pipeline](../src/reviewnlp/data/preprocess.py), [models](../src/reviewnlp/baselines/), [evaluation](../src/reviewnlp/evaluation/) and a [FastAPI service](../src/reviewnlp/serving/). The [tests](../tests/) run on every push and pull request ([CI](../.github/workflows/ci.yml)). | |
| **SQL** | [Seven queries](../analysis/complaint_trends/sql/) on hotels, reviews and complaints: CTEs, joins, window functions (rolling means, shares within partitions), a same-month year-over-year comparison and a within-hotel comparison with a standardised rate. They are [tested](../tests/test_complaint_trends.py) on a hand-built database with known answers. The [report](case_study/complaint_trends.md) runs them twice on all 515K reviews. The first run found a step in February 2016 that lifts every topic, possibly a change in how reviews were collected or recorded, and two family tags that swap. The second run compares only months after the step. Bathroom, air conditioning and pests are worth a look but not confirmed. Cleanliness and responsiveness are not yet findings, because the lexicon is right in only about half of their sampled quotes. Bed does not rise faster than other topics. | The lexicon's precision and recall, measured by two people in a pilot of 300 texts. A breakdown by hotel and city. |
| **Data management and reproducibility** | [`fetch_booking_515k.py`](../scripts/fetch_booking_515k.py) downloads the raw file from a pinned source and refuses it unless its size and SHA-256 match the Kaggle download. Deduplication runs before splitting, and every split directory has a manifest with hashes and overlap checks. The [three rebuilt splits](experiments/results/v2_splits/) record the raw file's hash, their fingerprints, date ranges, hotels and zero overlap. | |
| **Model development and validation** | Models are chosen on dev only ([classical baselines](../src/reviewnlp/baselines/classical.py)). Results carry a [bootstrap interval](../src/reviewnlp/evaluation/metrics.py), and pairs of models are compared with an [exact McNemar test](../src/reviewnlp/evaluation/significance.py). Three test sets ask different questions: random, [out-of-time](../configs/baselines_time.yaml) and [unseen hotels](../configs/baselines_hotel.yaml). The [results](experiments/results/split_views.json) show that later reviews score 1.7 macro-F1 points lower, while unseen hotels show similar observed performance in this comparison (not a test of equivalence). The aspect demo is evaluated on three label sets, with Wilson intervals ([`eval_space_triage.py`](../scripts/eval_space_triage.py)). | DistilBERT on the clean and out-of-time splits, with saved predictions, cost and a short decision note. |
| **Statistics** | Wilson and bootstrap intervals. A hotel-level bootstrap, since reviews of one hotel are not independent. A stricter interval when 29 topics are tested at once. Standardisation to separate a change in the hotel mix from a real change. A topic's gain on the other topics, net of the growth in topic mentions. The precision fall that would erase a rise, with a hypothetical Beta sensitivity analysis kept apart from the answer. A sensitivity bound for a shift in guests' nationality, not a test. | |
| **Honest evaluation** | Legacy results are kept, but [labelled](../README.md#historical-results-legacy-split-text-overlap) as measured on a test set that shares texts with training. Labels written by the AI assistant are [declared as such](../spaces/hotel-ops-demo/README.md). The project owner labelled 40 demo reviews without seeing the output, and every disagreement was analysed. The complaint report states what is not known (the cause of the February 2016 step, the lexicon's recall) and records a correction to its own reading of the quotes. | A second annotator and an agreement measure (Cohen's kappa): the [pilot](annotation/pilot_protocol.md) is ready to label, with its rules locked. |
| **Working with a user** | The project owner, as a user of the demo, reviewed 42 findings on six apartment reviews. Each error became an [acceptance test](../data/eval/space_triage_user6.json) and was fixed ([tests](../tests/test_space_demo.py)). | A timed pilot with and without the tool. |
| **Communication** | An English [README](../README.md), a Greek-language demo for hotel owners ([`spaces/hotel-ops-demo`](../spaces/hotel-ops-demo/)), and quotes shown next to every finding. | A five-minute case study in English. |

**Not covered by this project:** financial mathematics, credit-risk modelling
(PD models, calibration, stability), R and SAS.
